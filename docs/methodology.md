# Methodology

CSQE evaluates semantic contribution, relevance, duplication/repetition, emoji-only or extremely short content and other noise indicators. Records remain stored; high-confidence aggregates exclude or down-weight low scores.

Sentiment uses available topic, parent and nearby-reply context. Outputs separate sentiment, stance and emotion and always carry confidence. English, Hindi and Hinglish are first-class architecture targets. A future MuRIL provider requires task-specific fine-tuning or classification logic.

Audience intelligence is aggregate. Language is relatively high feasibility. Geography is observed where explicit and otherwise probabilistic. Age is only a broad probabilistic bracket. Exact age, income, address and weak gender inference are excluded.

Trend status uses volume growth and acceleration across timestamp windows. Network statements describe observed relationships and amplification, not proof of causation or true narrative origin.
